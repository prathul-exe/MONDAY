Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MONDAY Smart Attendance Tracker collects only the account and academic information you provide: your name, email address, optional student ID, department, semester, subjects, attendance logs, timetable entries, and academic deadlines.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to calculate attendance percentages, recovery paths, safe absence limits, deadline estimates, and to synchronize your schedule across your signed-in devices.

3. Data Isolation & Security

Passwords are stored exclusively as salted cryptographic hashes. Every database record is scoped to your user account and protected by authentication and access-control policies. We never sell or share your personal academic data with third parties.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated attendance, subject, timetable, and deadline records at any time from Settings → Account & Data → Delete Account.
